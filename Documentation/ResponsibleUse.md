# Responsible Use

Roam Control is intended for location-based app development, quality assurance and personal testing on an iPhone you own and control.

## Appropriate uses

- Testing how your own app behaves in another region.
- Reproducing a location-dependent bug.
- Validating map, geofence or route interfaces.
- Demonstrating a location flow on your own device.
- Personal experimentation that does not affect another person or service.

## Do not use Roam Control to

- Access a service, offer or feature you are not entitled to use.
- Evade a platform's safety, moderation, regional or anti-abuse controls.
- Falsify evidence, attendance, travel, delivery or emergency information.
- Mislead, monitor, harass or impersonate another person.
- Interfere with games, marketplaces or other services in breach of their rules.
- Control a device without the owner's informed permission.

Location simulation can affect every app using the iPhone's reported location. Stop the session and confirm that the real location has returned before using navigation, emergency, safety, transport or location-sharing features.
